Privacy Policy – ATLAS World Clock

Developer: five3 · Effective date: 2 October 2026

ATLAS World Clock is a watch face for Wear OS smartwatches, published on Google Play under the package name com.five3.atlas. This policy explains what information the watch face uses and how it is handled.

The short version

ATLAS World Clock does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data. It has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access. Everything it displays is computed on your watch and never leaves it.

Information the watch face displays

ATLAS World Clock is built with the Watch Face Format and contains no executable code of its own. The Wear OS system renders it and supplies the values it shows:

  • Time, date and time zone – taken from your watch's clock settings, used to draw the clock, the UTC readout and the day/night map. The map is calculated from the date and time only; the watch face does not access your location.
  • Steps, heart rate and battery level – provided by the Wear OS watch face runtime from the watch's own sensors and battery status, purely to draw the meters on the dial. Wear OS may ask you to allow the watch face to show sensor data when you first apply it; you can withdraw that permission at any time in your watch's settings.
  • Complication – the watch face has one customisable slot. If you fill it with data from another app (for example last night's sleep from Samsung Health), that app provides the data and its own privacy policy governs it. ATLAS World Clock only displays what you have chosen to show.

None of this information is recorded by the watch face, and none of it is sent anywhere.

Permissions

The watch face declares no Android permissions. Any permission prompt you see when applying it comes from the Wear OS system, which controls access to sensor data on your behalf.

Children

ATLAS World Clock is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the watch face changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. The current version is always available at this address.
